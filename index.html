How this works
🌿
Today — your daily view
Pick your day state. See what is assigned for today. Check things off. That is all this asks of you.
📋
Schedule — assign chores to days
Check the boxes for which days each chore belongs to. Leave a chore unassigned and it becomes flexible — you can show it any day.
✏️
My Chores — make it yours
Add anything. Delete anything. The list is pre-populated to get you started — change every single thing if you want.
〰️
My Wave — the pattern, no judgment
30 days of your day states as a chart. It does not tell you what it means. You get to decide that.

No streaks. No missed-day marks. No reminders. No shame.
